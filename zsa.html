The Z Scale Assessment (ZSA)

Where does your team sit on the Z Scale — from Zombie to Zealot?

The ZTI is a quick pulse check on one person. The ZSA is the full physical for a team — a deeper, twenty-question read on the conditions that decide whether a team stays half-dead or comes fully alive.

It measures five drivers: Flow, Feedback, and Continuous Learning — Gene Kim's Three Ways — plus Autonomy and Purpose from Daniel Pink's AMP. Four statements under each. Rate your team as it actually operates, from 1 (Never) to 5 (Always).
