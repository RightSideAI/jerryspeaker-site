The Zealot Time Audit (ZTA)

List the tasks you actually logged this week. Tag each one — the audit builds the honest picture, and shows how far it sits from what you'd have guessed.

Log a real week in 30-minute blocks first. Then enter the tasks below and tag each by its type of work and what to do with it (DRIP). The audit stacks four lenses — the four types of work, the DRIP matrix, your Z-Scale capacity, and flow efficiency — to reveal your true productive time, the capacity hiding in plain sight, and the revenue waiting on the other side of it.
