The ZTA at team scale — how much of your team's week actually creates value, and the capacity hiding in plain sight.

Same engine as the Zealot Time Audit, run across a whole team. The first question sets everything: is this team the constraint, or not? Because the right move for a bottleneck team is the opposite of the right move for a non-bottleneck one — and getting that backwards is why most "efficiency" drives quietly destroy throughput.
