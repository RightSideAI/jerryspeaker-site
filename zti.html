The Zealot Transformation Index (ZTI)

Where do you fall on the Z Scale — from Zombie to Zealot Leader?

Every person shows up to their work somewhere on a spectrum. At one end is the Zombie, beaten down by the system and running on the bare minimum. At the other is the Zealot Leader, whose greatest contribution is no longer what they build, but the Zealots they create in others.

The ten questions are built on Daniel Pink's model of what truly drives us — Autonomy, Mastery, and Purpose. Where you land on the Z Scale is a reflection of how alive those three drives are in your work right now.
