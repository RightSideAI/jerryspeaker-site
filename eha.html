The Environment Health Assessment (EHA)

How well you think you build the environment — versus how your team actually experiences it. The gap is the signal.

Leaders rate how well they build each of the Five Ideals of a healthy work environment. The team rates the same leader on the same five. When the two views diverge, that gap is the most useful number in leadership — because you can't fix an environment you can't see clearly. Rate each Ideal 1 (never) to 5 (always).
